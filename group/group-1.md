# 🖼️ Group 1: Cultural Heritage Dataset

[← Back to overview](../README.md)

**About the study:** The study analysed 100 digitised early 20th-century protest posters held by a local museum. It found that the posters drew on a small set of recurring images and slogans, that their style shifted from text-heavy designs towards bold, image-led designs over the period, and that many were produced by printers and artists who are no longer identifiable.
